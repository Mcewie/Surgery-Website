Sealed bid process

Sealed bid terms

These terms govern registration and the submission of sealed bids for the freehold of Riverbank, Westcott (title SY607273). Please read them before you apply.

1. Who runs the process

1.1 The process is run by To confirm — appointed agent or solicitor (“the Agent”), acting for the owner of the property (“the Seller”).

1.2 The freehold is registered in the name of a pension trustee. To confirm — registered proprietor and the Seller's authority to sell

2. Eligibility and registration

2.1 Only approved bidders may receive the bid pack or submit a bid. To be approved, an applicant must:

  • complete the registration form;
  • pass the Agent's identity and anti-money-laundering checks;
  • provide proof of funds, to the Agent's satisfaction, at the level they intend to bid;
  • pay the registration fee and the bid deposit.

2.2 The Agent may decline or withdraw approval where checks are incomplete or unsatisfactory, or where these terms are breached. To confirm — whether the fee and deposit are returned if an applicant is not approved

3. Registration fee

3.1 A registration fee of £200 is payable on approval. It covers verification and administration and is non-refundable, including if the process is withdrawn or the property is not sold. To confirm — solicitor to approve the non-refundable wording and VAT treatment

4. Bid deposit

4.1 A bid deposit of £1,000 is payable on approval and held in the Agent's client account.

4.2 The deposit is refundable. To confirm — refund timing (for example, within 10 working days of the decision), treatment for the successful bidder (credited against the purchase price or returned), and any circumstances in which it may be retained

5. The bid pack

5.1 Approved bidders get access to the data room. Its contents are confidential and may be used only to evaluate a bid. Bidders may share them only with their professional advisers and funders.

5.2 Bidders must rely on their own inspection, enquiries and professional advice. In particular, they should check the occupation position, the planning position (including condition 5 of permission MO/89/1022) and the title.

6. Submitting a bid

6.1 Bids must be made on the official bid form from the data room and received by To confirm — closing date and time.

6.2 Bids must be for a single fixed sum in pounds sterling. Escalating bids, or bids made by reference to other bids, will not be accepted.

6.3 Bids must be sent to To confirm — sealed bid address and email and marked “Riverbank, Westcott — sealed bid”. Bids are held unopened until the closing time.

6.4 Each bid must say how it is funded, list any conditions and give the bidder's solicitor's details.

7. After the closing date

7.1 The Seller is not bound to accept the highest or any bid. The Seller may consider certainty of funding, conditions and timing as well as price.

7.2 All bids are subject to contract. No binding agreement exists until contracts are exchanged. To confirm — or replace with the solicitor's approved wording if bids are to be binding

7.3 The Seller may extend the closing date, invite best and final bids from some or all bidders, or withdraw the property at any time. Every approved bidder will be notified of the outcome.

8. Conduct

8.1 Bidders must not collude with other bidders or try to find out the content of other bids. A breach may lead to disqualification.

9. Disclosures

9.1 The property is occupied by Dorking Medical Practice, and the occupation arrangements are disputed. Vacant possession is not offered or promised.

9.2 Under condition 5 of permission MO/89/1022, the premises may be used only for medical or health services. No consent for any other use is represented.

9.3 The parking area in front of the building is understood to lie outside the title boundary.

10. Law

10.1 These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
