Sealed bid registration

Apply to register as a bidder

Registration is limited to parties who can show they have the funds to proceed. Once approved, you'll get access to the full bid pack and can submit a sealed bid.

  1. Apply using this form.
  2. Verify. The agent confirms your identity and proof of funds.
  3. Pay the £200 registration fee (non-refundable) and the £1,000 bid deposit (refundable) to the agent's client account.
  4. Access the secure data room and bid form.

Proof of funds

Please don't attach bank statements or ID to the email this form creates. After you apply, the agent will send you a secure way to provide:

  • photo ID and proof of address (for each individual, or for the directors and owners of a company);
  • evidence of funds at the level you intend to bid, such as a recent bank or solicitor's letter, or a mortgage agreement in principle with evidence of the balance.
