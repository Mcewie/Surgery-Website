Privacy notice

How your information is handled when you apply to register as a bidder or send an enquiry about Riverbank, Westcott.

Who handles your information

Applications and enquiries are handled by To confirm — agent's firm name, address and contact, who runs the sealed bid process for the owner. The website's forms open an email in your own email app, and the website doesn't store what you type.

What it is used for

  • To assess your application to register as a bidder.

Proof of funds and identity documents

Please don't send these by open email. The agent will give you a secure way to provide them. To confirm — the agent's secure upload or verification method

Data room access

If you're approved, your email address is added to the list of people allowed into the data room. Access uses an email one-time code provided by Cloudflare. Your access is removed when the process ends.

Wider marketing

You'll only receive information about other properties if you tick the separate, optional box. You can withdraw that choice at any time.

How long it is kept, and your rights

To confirm — the agent's retention periods (anti-money-laundering records are normally kept for five years), lawful basis and data-protection contact.

You can ask the agent for a copy of the information they hold about you, or ask them to correct or delete it, subject to their legal obligations. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
