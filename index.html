Alternative-use potential

An alternative use, including residential, may be worth investigating if occupation is lawfully resolved and replacement medical provision is delivered. This is potential for a purchaser to assess. It would require planning permission, including relief from condition 5 of MO/89/1022, and would be assessed against Green Belt, Surrey Hills and neighbourhood plan policy.

Relocation would not itself grant permission for another use. A 2021 application for a single dwelling was withdrawn. No residential consent, and no route to one, is represented in these particulars.

How to bid

The sealed bid process

A single, confidential round of bids from verified parties. Every bidder gets the same information and the same deadline, and no bids are disclosed to other bidders.

  1. 1

    Apply to register

    Complete the online application with your details, how you're buying and your solicitor.

  2. 2

    Verification

    The agent checks your identity (anti-money-laundering) and your proof of funds for the level you intend to bid. Only verified parties go forward.

  3. 3

    Fee and deposit

    You pay the £200 registration fee and the £1,000 bid deposit to the agent's client account.

  4. 4

    Data room access

    Once you're approved, your email address is given access to the secure data room with the full bid pack and bid form.

  5. 5

    Submit a sealed bid

    Send your bid on the official bid form by the closing date. Bids stay sealed until the deadline passes.

  6. 6

    Decision

    The seller considers all compliant bids together and notifies every bidder of the outcome.
