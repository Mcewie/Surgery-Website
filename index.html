Investment

Investment opportunity

Riverbank offers buyers an opportunity to assess an existing commercial property alongside its longer-term potential. Dorking Medical Practice has supported proposals for alternative premises, although relocation has not been secured.

If suitable replacement provision is delivered and the occupation position is resolved, a purchaser could investigate residential conversion or another appropriate use. Buyers should assess the opportunity against the current planning position and the information in the legal pack.
